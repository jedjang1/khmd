TRANSFORMATIONS: KHMD linked addresses to unique parcel candidates, checked valid
geometry and interior representative points, transformed from the documented
source CRS to EPSG:5179 using x=easting/y=northing, and assigned the project grid
KHMD_UTMK_1KM_V1, origin(0,0), projected1,000m squares. It aggregated repeated
observations by grid, contract quarter and family. Parcel geometries, PNU and
representative-point coordinates are not distributed. This is reference-based
research processing, not surveying, official approval, endorsement or warranty.
It does not establish historical contract-time parcel locations or SGIS/national
standard-grid identity. Source licence and warranty notices must remain intact.
